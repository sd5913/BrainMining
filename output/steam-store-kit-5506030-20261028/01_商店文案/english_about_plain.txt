No inspiration yet? Try digging around inside your head.
The Game Jam theme is "Emergence." Time to get up and dig for an idea.

WOoOW is a first-person digging adventure set inside a mind. Pick up your tools, carve through rock, uncover curious objects, and step into unexpected rooms. Every encounter is another way to explore what emergence might mean.

Dig your own way forward
Swing your pickaxe and reshape the rock around you. Follow mineral deposits and clues in the environment, gather resources, and upgrade your tools. The way ahead is something you can uncover—and something you can make.

Discover rooms with unexpected rules
A tunnel might lead to an office full of persistent pop-ups, or a dark passage you navigate by striking its surfaces. Learn each room’s rules, encounter strange changes of scale, take part in a card confrontation, and puzzle over the connections between ideas.

Collect curious little thoughts
Look closely at the rock and the corners of each space. Follow the clues, dig out unusual objects, and add them to your collection journal. Rotate and inspect them: what do they remind you of? Each one is a small trace of your search for inspiration.

See an idea emerge
Keep moving, looking, and experimenting as familiar rooms give way to unfamiliar spaces. Rather than searching for one correct answer, you are playing through the process of an idea taking shape.

Features
• First-person digging: carve through rock, gather resources, and acquire and upgrade tools.
• Curious encounters and puzzles across handcrafted spaces with changing rules.
• Environmental clues, unusual collectibles, and an inspectable collection journal.
• Simple low-poly art, from warm rooms to shadowy tunnels and surreal spaces.
• A single-player journey guided by curiosity and the search for inspiration.
